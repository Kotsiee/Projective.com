## 1. Executive summary

**Verdict (FCA, 2026-10-29): the core ledger is sound and the money doors are mostly honest, but the
domain is not production-ready.** The append-only ledger with loud primitives, the trigger-enforced
"no hold, no work" gate, the webhook-only settlement with in-transaction idempotency, and the
pending window are production-grade. Around them are two security holes that PostgREST can reach
today, one path where a card can be charged and never credited, a paid product (consultations)
delivered free, a revenue line (marketplace commission) never charged, and a ring of automation that
saves settings and never runs. FA (2026-10-17) read the same ledger as "sound … the work here is
coverage, not redesign" (§4.5).

**Since 2026-10-17 (FCA):** none of the prior audit's P0 items was fixed. Six findings changed in
part (A16, CO18, K2, K3, K4, W4). Five design items were fixed or superseded (D6, D11, D16, D18,
D28). Thirty-four new findings were logged (N1–N34).

**Since 2026-10-29 (doc-derived):** the owner ruled D-1–D-13 and FA's flags on 2026-10-30 (§2). Of
the 195 register IDs (§5–§7), 42 are Fixed (39 of them since 10-29, by Waves 0–2 and the Wave 1.1
doc sweep), 16 Partly fixed, 23 Ruled and awaiting their build, 57 Open, 4 Planned, 2 Superseded, 1
Unverified and 50 Compliant; the commits are in §11. **Open P0 now:** C13 (FBP SV5 → UI5); the
island halves of V6 and R10 (FBP UI2); N2's money path (SCH-WP6, owned by SCA); FA's P0 UI gates D1,
D2, D7 (FBP UI1, UI3).

## 4. Ledger integrity — the migrated single-entry model vs the planned double-entry model

### 4.1 How money moves today

```text
Card ─► Stripe platform balance (Separate Charges & Transfers; no transfer_data)
          │
          ▼ webhook payment_intent.succeeded → settle_card_payment
     payer wallet  +topup (Available at once)
          │ escrow_hold  (fn_wallet_debit; payer balance falls)
          ▼
     finance.escrows row  status='held'          ← the money exists ONLY as this row
          │ release (approval · negative consent · fair exit · dispute split)
          ▼
     finance.pending_releases  amount + bonus − fee   (no ledger line yet)
          │ fn_mature_pending_releases after pending_release_days (7)
          ▼
     payee wallet  +escrow_release (Available)
          │ begin_payout  (debit) → Stripe Transfer → connected account → bank
          ▼
     fee: recorded on escrows.platform_fee_cents · credited NOWHERE
```

- **Balances.** `wallets.balance_cents` is a cache of Available, written only by `fn_wallet_credit`
  / `fn_wallet_debit` beside a `transactions` row carrying `balance_after_cents` (F1200:10-56).
  Locked, Pending and On hold are projections from `escrows` and `pending_releases`
  (`svc/live-wallet.ts:256-265`).
- **`transactions.reason`** is free text with no enum or CHECK (T17:43-80), and
  **`transactions.fund_state` is never written**: every row, `escrow_hold` included, reads
  `available` (N13, against the T17:56-65 comment).

### 4.4 What the single-entry model hides

Each defect below would show up as an imbalance in a double-entry trial balance, and none is caught
today:

- **CO20**: credit without cash.
- **N4**: release of an unfunded bonus.
- **S5**: fee retained but unbooked.
- **N13**: `fund_state` wrong on every row.
- **W4**: a chargeback loss absorbed off-ledger.

A per-currency **conservation identity** is computable from existing tables without changing any
writer:

```text
Σ settled inbound payments + Σ on-account credit − Σ completed payouts (net of reversals)
  − Σ chargeback recoveries
  ≟ Σ wallets.balance_cents + Σ escrows(held|disputed).amount_cents
    + Σ pending_releases(pending).(amount − recovered) + Σ platform fees retained
    − Σ deadline bonuses released + Σ unrecovered chargebacks
```

The recommended staging (identity view now, a shadow journal before scale, no big-bang rewrite) is
Decision **D-5** (§2.3). **Status:** Ruled → M2 A; Open → FBP DB16
(`finance.v_conservation_identity`, probe `conservation_identity.sql`, an ops reader with L5).

**Derived from the code (2026-11-12, the DB16 / DB8 / DB9 session; nothing built).** The draft needs
three corrections. (1) The external terms come from the event tables, never the ledger rows, or a
credit with no cash (CO20's shape) balances itself. In: settled
`inbound_payments.amount_received_cents` with a `transaction_id`, plus on-account orders'
`total_minor` (`status = 'invoiced'`). Out: payouts' `amount_cents − reversed_cents` unless `failed`
or `cancelled`, `inbound_payments.refunded_cents`, lost chargebacks' full `amount_cents`, and paid
invoices' `amount_due_cents` (they settle the on-account receivable). (2) The bank takes a lost
chargeback's whole disputed amount, so "out" carries all of it and the right-hand side subtracts
only what no wallet covered (lost chargebacks' `unrecovered_cents` and
`inbound_payments.refund_unrecovered_cents`); the draft's recovery out plus unrecovered in counts
the loss twice. (3) No bonus term: a released bonus nobody funded is the imbalance the identity
exists to show (N4; DB12 now refuses one). The fees are released escrows' `platform_fee_cents` and
`orders.platform_fee_minor`, and pending counts `pending` and `on_hold` net of `recovered_cents`.
The seed does not satisfy it (N35), and the simulator's rows get no class (#423 (A)); FBP DB16
carries the classes and the terms.

### 4.5 The money-path invariant set (FA §4.1)

The ledger is sound: append-only, loud primitives, idempotency claimed in the effect's own
transaction, SKIP LOCKED maturation, held-only flips. The work here is coverage, not redesign. Add
one property probe (`supabase/probes/finance_invariants.sql`) that, for every wallet, asserts
`balance_cents = Σ signed ledger = latest balance_after_cents`,
`Σ pots.balance_cents ≤
balance_cents`, and that every `pending_releases` row in `pending`/`on_hold`
has no matching credit yet. Run it in every finance prompt's acceptance.

**Status:** Fixed (`172f9d66`: the probe asserts all three; FBP's standard header keeps it green in
every prompt).

## 8. Design notes kept from FA §4 and §3.4

### 8.1 Wallet scope honesty (`?w=`) — FA §4.3

A `w` the viewer cannot open must never silently become another wallet. Rule: the route compares
requested with resolved and **303s** to the resolved address (viewer-dependent, so not cacheable),
drops `do` and `back`, and carries `?notice=wallet-unavailable`. This mirrors the console's
`forbidden → /teams?notice=` pattern (TEAMS_AUDIT §4.1).

**Status:** Fixed (FBP SV2 `d4c4a194`; ROUTING.md's wallet row documents the 303s and the notice).

### 8.2 Enlarge → modal → `/inspect` — FA §4.4

Nothing in the wallet enlarges today, and `/inspect/[fileId]` is a `files.items` viewer. _Proposal:_
do **not** route finance PDFs through `/inspect`, because they are generated documents and not
files. Keep fetch-then-save (`saveBlob`). If an enlarge pattern is wanted, it is one `Dialog`
(replace in place, §B.10.9) that hosts the same `AreaChart` / `DataTable` at full width, opened from
a tier-5 "Expand" row action, with no third-level hand-off.

**Ruled:** ruling E3 FA (h) ⭐ (#337): an optional Expand opens one Dialog; no `/inspect`. **Open
→** FBP UI4 (step 7). SBP-A cites this single-`Dialog` proposal as its pattern.
